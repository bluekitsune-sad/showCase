I recommend having 12 portfolio projects in total: 2 projects for each of your 6 categories.

Each category should have two different audiences:

- Customer Showcase: A visually attractive, easy-to-understand product that demonstrates what you can build for individuals, startups, and small businesses.
- Business / Enterprise Showcase: A more advanced system that demonstrates your ability to build scalable applications, automate operations, manage data, and solve organizational problems.

The important thing is that these shouldn't just be 12 random CRUD projects. Each should solve a recognizable problem and give visitors a reason to contact you.
